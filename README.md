# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition and workforce demographics to help HR teams identify trends, risks, and improvement areas.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand employee attrition patterns
- Identify high-risk demographics and job roles
- Support data-driven HR decisions

## 📊 Key Metrics
- Total Employees: 1470 
- Attrition Count: 238
- Attrition Rate: 20.8%  
- Average Age: 37  
- Average Salary: 6.9K  
- Average Years at Company: 7  

## 🔍 Dashboard Insights
- Attrition by Age Group, Gender, Education, and Job Role
- Higher attrition observed among Sales Executives
- Employees with lower salary slabs show higher attrition
- Most attrition occurs in early years of employment

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- Power BI
- DAX Measures
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- HR Analytics & KPI Design


## 📈 Outcome
This dashboard enables HR stakeholders to proactively address attrition and improve employee retention strategies.
